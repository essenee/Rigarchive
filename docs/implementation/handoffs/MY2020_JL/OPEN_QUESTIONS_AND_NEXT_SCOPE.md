Created: 2026-11-08

## Review scope

Review the 49 research rows (15 two-door, 34 four-door) and the 21 proposed
body/marketed-grade variants. Approve the intended grade labels and source scope
before authorizing Candidate 2.1.0 construction. No candidate, correspondence
proposal/publication, Matrix certification or import plan was produced.

The recommended construction scope is exactly the 49 positive observed offerings,
not every trim crossed with every engine. Use the retained March 3 two-door and
four-door guides as a coherent primary source pair: together their populated
cells cover all 49 observed combinations. Preserve earlier/later revision
comparisons and corroboration, including April omissions. This does not certify
that an order could be placed throughout the whole model year or that every
listed combination was delivered.

## Grade identity decisions

Recommend separate marketed grades for each named QOP column, including Black and
Tan, Willys, Willys Sport, Freedom, Rubicon Recon, Sport Altitude, Sahara Altitude,
North Edition and Sahara High Altitude in their actually offered bodies.
These have named offering columns, distinct model-scoped QOP suffixes and factory
content tables, rather than an arbitrary accessory name or transmission choice.
This follows the existing source-established-grade boundary; no new grade entity
or W2 equipment architecture is proposed.

The final spelling/expansion of the proposed canonical trim labels needs review:
`Black and Tan`, `Rubicon Recon`, `Sahara High Altitude`, and `North Edition` are
proposed normalization choices. Four-door names retain the established Unlimited
prefix. Willys Sport and Willys are separate source columns; do not merge them.
The Sport-family and Sahara-family Altitude suffix `L` is not globally unique;
retain `(source model code, QOP)` as the native offering scope.

No separate baseline should arise solely from manual versus automatic, price,
paint, tops, an optional equipment group or engine-code spelling. The actual
structured powertrain facts must support any engine distinction.

## Classification gates

Fresh evidence-backed classification review is needed for:

- ERC: aspiration and electrification, covering 17 rows.
- ERG: aspiration, covering 3 rows.
- EC1: electrification, covering 15 rows.
- EXJ: electrification, covering 8 rows.

The proposed naturally aspirated/nonhybrid values are not approved. Existing
MY2019 approvals must not be extended. Candidate construction, if authorized,
may retain those fields null until supporting evidence and exact adjudications
are approved under the existing workflow. First strengthen conventional hardware
or explicit classification support where needed, especially EXJ nonhybrid.
No source assertion should claim manufacturer wording that is actually derived.

## Date and availability questions

April no longer lists Black and Tan in either body, nor EXJ for ordinary
four-door Rubicon. Keep the earlier positive offerings in the model-year union.
Those omissions do not prove permanent cancellation, production end dates or a
withdrawal of all delivered vehicles. The generic specification still lists
Rubicon diesel. For this discovery, these are revision-specific observations,
not a reason to fabricate dates or discard source evidence.

North Edition is present in the retained October 15 guide before its later public
announcement. Use the document's printed date as revision provenance only; do
not label it a retail launch or production date. Generic early specifications
omit later editions and are not an exhaustive trim matrix.
