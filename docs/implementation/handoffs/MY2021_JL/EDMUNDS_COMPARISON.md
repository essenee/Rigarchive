# Edmunds independent comparison

Created: 2026-11-08
Status: Proposed

Manufacturer-issued guides remain primary. Edmunds is secondary corroboration;
its optional-engine lists do not replace exact guide applicability or approve a
canonical classification.

The [Wrangler trim comparison](https://www.edmunds.com/jeep/wrangler/2021/trims/)
contains 22 conventional body/trim labels, including Sport RHD and Rubicon 392.
The separate [4xe comparison](https://www.edmunds.com/jeep/wrangler-4xe/2021/trims/)
contains Sahara, Rubicon and High Altitude. After preserving four-door and 4xe
context in the normalized names, these match all 25 proposed labels.

Individual style URLs, observed engine descriptions and section locators are
retained in evidence/edmunds_observations.json. Twenty conventional specification
pages were inspected through browser Engine and expanded Mechanical Options
sections; High Altitude and 392 tables were available through the public web
reader after browser access became restricted. The 4xe three-column comparison
was available through the public web reader. No restriction was bypassed.
These are retained research transcriptions; no original HTML-byte hash is claimed.

Every one of the 74 manufacturer-supported rows has described engine-family
corroboration in edmunds_comparison.json. There are zero unmatched manufacturer
rows and zero identified secondary-only configurations in this comparison.
Engine sales codes remain established by the primary guide, not Edmunds.

The comparisons distinguish ordinary manual 3.6 V6 from the optional eTorque V6;
2.0 turbo, 3.0 turbo diesel and 6.4 V8 remain distinct. ERC's later automatic
pairings do not require additional VehicleDefinitions, and this corroboration
does not independently certify each such pairing or every five-field tuple.
Rubicon diesel appears in the Unlimited Rubicon optional-engine list; unlike the
MY2020 comparison, its MY2021 omission is not observed.

Naming/classification differences: `80TH` is expanded to 80th Anniversary Edition;
High Altitude retains Sahara-family provenance while using its marketed label;
Edmunds calls editions subtrim packages but lists them separately. Those terms do
not override RigArchive's manufacturer-grade rules. Edmunds uses separate 4xe
and 392 navigation/catalogue surfaces; RigArchive keeps existing Wrangler JL
Generation. RHD is listed, but that does not solve the canonical steering issue.

Edmunds includes production-end qualifiers on special editions. Those are not
adopted as build dates: retained guides stop listing Islander/80th/Freedom at
other observation points. This is a temporal-coverage difference, not grounds to
remove an earlier-supported configuration. Optional equipment, trim prices and
market inventory widgets are not factory identity evidence.
