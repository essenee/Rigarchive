## Why these are more than QOP-number differences

Sport S is a named manufacturer column and appears separately in the manufacturer's
MY2021 feature-availability matrix (page 1). Willys and Willys Sport have separate
named QOP columns and content, with distinct Edmunds listings; the shared Sport
model code does not merge manufacturer-recognized grades. Sport/Sahara/Rubicon
retain their established manufacturer family identity.

Freedom and Islander are named edition columns with their own content/badging:
September 29 two-door page 3 and four-door page 3 identify Freedom Edition content
and Islander seats/decals/plaques. The 80TH EDITION two-door / 80TH four-door columns
have 80th-anniversary-tagged equipment. Expanding those labels to 80th Anniversary
Edition is an explicit naming recommendation, supported by independent listings,
not a fabricated full source quote. Earlier positive availability is retained.

ALTITUDE must stay scoped to its Sport or Sahara model heading. High Altitude is
named in the Sahara-family column, has High Altitude badging/package content and
is marketed separately. Normalize to Unlimited High Altitude, retaining its Sahara
family. Four-door 4xe page 23 in the June 9 guide explicitly associates JLXP74
29P with the Sahara 4XE heading and 29N with High Altitude/badging. Keep Unlimited
Sahara 4xe, Unlimited High Altitude 4xe and Unlimited Rubicon 4xe as the proposed
marketed names. Do not also construct a bare Sahara/High Altitude/Rubicon ECX row
that duplicates the same offering under a different name.

Rubicon 392 has an explicit model heading, JLJX74/27X/ESG and production technical
material. It is a marketed performance variant within Wrangler JL, not a separate
model or Generation. The production release's launch description does not create
another Launch Edition baseline absent an independent grade distinction.

The manufacturer may implement marketed grades as quick-order packages. The
accepted distinction is evidence-backed marketed grade identity, not whether the
word package occurs. Conversely, roof/door/towing/color choices, transmissions,
Islander Plus and Xtreme Recon AGB do not establish extra marketed grades here.
AGB is a selectable factory package with scoped ERG/392 applicability, not the
MY2020 Rubicon Recon grade carried into MY2021.

Authority: ADR-0005 grade/market semantics, ADR-0010 package/transmission
subordination and approved Wrangler body naming, ADR-0011's preservation of that
normalization, and ADR-0012/RA-044 powertrain identity. No rules are changed.
