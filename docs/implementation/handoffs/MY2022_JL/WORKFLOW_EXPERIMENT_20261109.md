## 2026-11-10 change of course — W2 Edmunds-first provisional ingestion

**Owner direction:** For the remainder of Wrangler ingestion, starting with MY2022, use **Edmunds as the primary provisional working source** for marketed trim/body taxonomy, engines, transmissions, Mechanical Options and Quick Order Packages. The owner judges it likely correct or mostly correct based on favorable Wrangler comparisons so far. This is a pragmatic confidence assumption, **not proof of manufacturer-level accuracy**. A later, more comprehensive manufacturer ordering-guide validation is desirable if usage, priorities and time permit; it is **not a prerequisite to ordinary W2 progress**. This direction supersedes the experiment's earlier manufacturer-discovery-first emphasis for *future work*, without rewriting the actual historical experiment results below.

**Working method:** Read trim-specific Mechanical Options and Packages as well as the selected/default engine/transmission shown in Edmunds trim headings. Do not mistake a displayed base configuration for the only available one. Where Edmunds explicitly links a QOP, engine and transmission, retain that relationship and its source reference. Where options are only co-listed, record availability but **do not invent pairings or Cartesian products**. Reuse the existing 55 candidates and retained manufacturer evidence; fill or revise only supportable attributes, mark all Edmunds-derived claims as Edmunds-supported/provisionally accepted, and investigate ambiguities or conflicts selectively. Existing manufacturer guides can corroborate or resolve targeted questions but do not require exhaustive edition-by-edition reconciliation.

**Evidence boundaries:** “Edmunds-supported” does not mean manufacturer-verified. “Provisionally accepted” is an explicit project evidence-policy choice, not a claim that all configuration details have been checked. “Deferred manufacturer validation” is not completed validation. Preserve retrieval dates, exact links/style identifiers, research transcription versus retained original bytes, source lineage and contradictions. Do not assert independent corroboration from the same underlying data or silently reclassify a research transcription as original publisher content. Source availability and source reliability remain separate questions.

**Governance boundary:** This is an ingestion **workflow** called W2, distinct from RigArchive's already-named W2 full-specifications architecture. It changes research priority and provisional source acceptance, **not** Candidate 2.1.0, Engine Proof 2.0, the five-field identity contract, eligibility, human classification/grade adjudication, source-bound generation correspondence, Matrix certification, stale-plan validation, atomic create-only imports or audit receipts. If existing contracts cannot admit a provisionally Edmunds-supported assertion, report the exact blocker and seek a separate owner-approved decision; do not forge a proof or relax validation in this documentation task.

**Reason for change:** The initial 55-candidate experiment left 0 complete and 0 eligible candidates, with 112 unresolved field instances, 13 ERC/eTorque peer questions, eight transmission/QOP review items and 72 missing generation-correspondence keys. Further exhaustive manufacturer reconciliation was judged disproportionate to the current objective. No completed W2 imports, verified accuracy rate, time/token savings or proven end-to-end efficiency are claimed. Retain the original experiment's measurements and evaluate actual outcomes later.

Known Sport RHD consumer-scope deferrals for **MY2021 and MY2022** are tracked in the [Canonical Coverage and Deferrals Register](../../CANONICAL_COVERAGE_AND_DEFERRALS.md). Neither is assumed fleet-only or permanently excluded.

## Decision and rationale

For MY2022, reuse the initial manufacturer discovery inventory and targeted
Edmunds offering comparison to construct incomplete provisional candidates.
Investigate material discrepancies and identity questions as they arise; do not
repeat comprehensive manufacturer acquisition or broad historical revision research.

The owner reports substantial Codex processing time and token consumption during
MY2019–MY2021 ingestion. No consistent quantitative baseline is available here.
Several manufacturer-authored ordering guides were acquired from forums or other
third-party hosts. Their authorship and hosting must remain separately identified:
third-party hosting presents a long-term availability risk, even for authentic
manufacturer content. Extensive investigation does not automatically guarantee
stronger durable provenance. Durable provenance depends on retained bytes,
qualified assertions, hashes, applicability and attributable decisions, not merely
on the number of searches, revisions or webpages visited.

The experiment seeks a proportionate balance among research effort, source quality,
canonical accuracy and maintainability. Reusing retained sources avoids renewed
reliance on transient hosts; it does not make a transcription equivalent to
original publisher bytes. The Edmunds reconciliation CSV is explicitly preserved
as a RigArchive research transcription, not mislabeled as archived Edmunds HTML.

## What changed

| Dimension | MY2019–MY2021 approach | MY2022 experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manufacturer evidence | Extensive revision-level acquisition and reconciliation | Initial discovery matrix using 21 manufacturer-origin sources; reuse rather than reacquire |
| Ordering-guide coverage | Broad historical revision investigation | 18 guides, nine revisions per body, with known coverage limitations |
| External corroboration | Detailed configuration-level corroboration | Edmunds offering-taxonomy comparison, including trim-specific engine/QOP option listings |
| Discrepancy resolution | Comprehensive investigation across source assertions | Targeted investigation of material discrepancies; retain unresolved findings |
| Configuration inventory | Extensive evidence normalization and adjudication | Start from 56 discovered offerings: 55 consumer proposals and one deferred RHD |
| Powertrain classification | Full evidence-backed five-field classification before eligibility | Allow incomplete provisional candidates; unsupported fields must be resolved before certification/eligibility |
| Source preservation | Detailed source retention and provenance | Reuse retained sources and existing lineage without repeated acquisition |
| Certification/import | Strict Matrix certification, atomic create-only import and audit receipts | Unchanged |

The difference is the timing and depth of research, not permission to certify
incomplete identities. Earlier milestones also allowed preparatory incomplete
artifacts under the contracts; their eventual eligibility required complete
classification. This record does not retroactively change those procedures or
claim they never had incomplete preparation stages.

## Evidence supporting this bounded trial

The [Edmunds comparison](edmunds_comparison_20261109/README.md) found counterparts
for **56/56 manufacturer offerings**: **45 same-name**, **11 equivalent-name**,
**zero unmatched baseline offerings** and **zero genuine contradictions identified**.
Eight manufacturer-supported transmission/QOP alternatives lacked Edmunds
corroboration; they remain retained review items, not deleted alternatives or
additional canonical identities. Offering-level agreement does **not** establish
complete five-field powertrain identities.

The [provisional construction checkpoint](lightweight_candidates_20261109/README.md)
contains 55 Candidate 2.1.0 artifacts (15 two-door / 40 four-door) and excludes
RHD. Consumer mappings are 44 same-name plus 11 equivalent-name; the remaining
same-name mapping belongs to deferred RHD. All six families remain represented.
Initial validation finds **0 complete, 55 incomplete, 55 blocked, 0 eligible**,
112 unresolved field instances, 13 uncertain ERC/eTorque peer pairs and 72 missing
source/model generation-correspondence keys. No Engine Proof 2.0 is supported yet.
These are current findings, not evidence of completed MY2022 ingestion.

## Mandatory safeguards — unchanged

- Canonical identity invariants and distinctions, including the five-field
  powertrain tuple; engine codes alone cannot establish equivalence/distinctness.
- Source lineage and attribution: raw references/hashes, exact source/model/QOP
  membership, commercial applicability and original-versus-derived assertions.
- Unsupported-value handling: preserve nulls and contradictions; no inferred
  defaults, Cartesian expansion, or false claims of complete evidence.
- Human approval for material classification/grade findings under the existing
  exact-candidate adjudication process; construction authorization is not approval.
- Candidate 2.1.0 and Engine Proof 2.0 contract validation. Unsupported proofs are
  not emitted; incomplete candidates cannot be forced into eligibility.
- Generation correspondence review and separately authorized publication. An
  intended PK/UUID is context and cannot substitute for reviewed source binding.
- Matrix certification and exact authorization, with complete assessed identities.
- Stale-plan and evidence/peer revalidation at the existing execution boundary.
- Atomic create-only import execution and model-year rollback safeguards.
- Import execution receipts, attributable decisions and preserved auditability.

No Handbook, Blueprint or accepted ADR was changed. Candidate, Engine Proof,
Manifest, Matrix, domain and source contracts remain unchanged. The exception is
identified in the [existing ingestion protocol](../../../development/INGESTION_PROTOCOL.md),
without rewriting MY2019–MY2021 records.

## Measurements and evaluation after MY2022 completion

The initial bounded script measured **1.257802 seconds** for candidate construction,
an independent in-process duplicate rebuild, contract checks and artifact output;
**0.143702 seconds** for read-only planning/readiness; and **1.875675 seconds** from
its instrumentation start to the final timing record. It wrote 55 candidate files
(8,111,439 bytes) and 128 assertion-set files (824,644 bytes). These are local script
measurements, not total chat/research/engineering time, model latency or token usage.
Independent repeat/negative-control verification is separately measured. There is
no supported percentage saving or total-token comparison.
