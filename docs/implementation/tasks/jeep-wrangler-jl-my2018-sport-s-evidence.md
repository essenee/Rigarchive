# MY2018 US Jeep Wrangler JL Sport S and powertrain evidence

## Director decision

Recognize **Sport S** as a factory grade for the two-door JL and **Unlimited Sport S** for the four-door JL. Jeep ordered Sport S through the Sport quick-order package structure. Preserve that ordering terminology in source lineage. The distinct marketed grade, corroborated across the sources below, supports the canonical trim distinction. This decision applies specifically to Sport S.

## Source discrepancy

This is the first J.D. Power model-year trim-listing omission identified in RigArchive's ingestion work to date. The MY2018 [Wrangler two-door trim listing](https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2018/jeep/wrangler/trim) and [Wrangler Unlimited trim listing](https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2018/jeep/wrangler-unlimited/trim) both omit Sport S. J.D. Power's own [2018 Wrangler review](https://www.jdpower.com/cars/expert-reviews/powersteering-2018-jeep-wrangler-review?make=jeep&model=wrangler) expressly names Sport S among the new JL trim levels. [Edmunds' 2018 Wrangler trim comparison](https://www.edmunds.com/jeep/wrangler/2018/trims/) lists Sport S and Unlimited Sport S separately from Sport and Unlimited Sport.

The director supplied two local PDFs, `March 22, 2018 2-door JL Order Guide.pdf` and `March 22, 2018 2-door JL Order Guide-2.pdf`, and identified Sport S in their Sport package tables. Local PDF access was blocked, so the two copies have no verified hashes or comparison in this review. The [public US two-door JL order-guide thread](https://www.jlwranglerforums.com/forum/threads/2018-jeep-wrangler-jl-2-door-order-guide-u-s.2212/) indexes the March 22 guide. The [March 22 US two-door JL order guide](https://www.jlwranglerforums.com/forum/attachments/march-22-2018-2-door-jl-order-guide-pdf.24077/#page=2) confirms the reissue date on page 1. Page 2 places Sport S in its own quick-order column under two-door Sport (model code `JLJL72`). It lists `23S` for the 3.6L V6 with six-speed manual, `24S` for the 3.6L V6 with eight-speed automatic, and `28S` for the 2.0L turbo eTorque with eight-speed automatic. This establishes both two-door Sport S engine configurations and their transmission availability. The public copy provides a verifiable citation for the March 22 revision. Its bytes were not compared to the local copies.

The two public links supplied in the follow-up point to the same [March 22, 2018 US four-door JLU order guide](https://www.jlwranglerforums.com/forum/attachments/march-22-2018-4-door-jlu-order-guide-pdf.24074/#page=2). Page 2 places Sport S in a separate quick-order column under Unlimited Sport (model code `JLJL74`). It lists `23S` for 3.6L V6 with six-speed manual, `24S` for 3.6L V6 with eight-speed automatic, and `28S` for 2.0L turbo eTorque with eight-speed automatic. This directly establishes both Unlimited Sport S engine configurations and their transmission availability. The public four-door PDF is a separate citation from the two named local PDFs.

### Director decision on Unlimited Moab

Recognize Unlimited Moab as a distinct MY2018 US JL canonical trim. FCA marketed it as a Sahara-based limited edition, while the August 8 guide documents a distinct Moab quick-order column and code `24M` under `JLJP74`. The director's classification reflects the marketed edition and its capability-oriented factory equipment. This decision applies only to Moab.

The August 8 guide, page 5, pairs `24M` with the 3.6L Pentastar V6 and eight-speed automatic. The package includes Selec-Trac full-time 4WD (`DHP`) and the anti-spin rear axle (`DSA`). FCA's launch release explicitly identifies the standard Trac-Lok limited-slip rear differential. The adjacent turbo code `28G` belongs to Sahara. Transmission, transfer case, and differential remain evidence on the Moab configuration. [August guide p. 5](https://www.jlwranglerforums.com/forum/attachments/2018_wrangler_4_door_jl_08_08_2018-pdf.76331/#page=5) · [FCA introduction](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeep-introduces-new-2018-wrangler-moab-edition-300703119.html)

The unchanged 14-row March inventory and one-row August supplement establish 15 supported configurations, one existing match (PK 715), and 14 prospective additions. The August guide directly places Moab under the same US MY2018 JL model code `JLJP74`. The proposed correspondence uses the exact shared March model-code evidence document plus the August Moab configuration document. Its applicability rests on the August model-code table. Publication and planner validation remain pending.
